01

Air it out.

Five minutes each, no interrupting. One of you says what you're carrying that nobody sees. The other one says it back until it's right. Then you switch.

Not a fight. A download.

02

Sort it out. The Load Out deck.

Every card is a job your house actually runs on, down to the invisible ones. Each card lands in one of three piles. The third pile is the one nobody else has.

03

Fold it in.

You fold a real basket of laundry together, side by side, hands busy, eyes forward. It's the easiest place to keep talking. Before the last towel, you pick your nightly check-in: same time, ten minutes, same three questions.

What landed on you today? What can I take? What's tomorrow?

The kids aren't parked. They're in a lab.

Down the hall, LifeSkills Lab runs a session of its own. Real expert, real hands-on challenge, and a star for every kid to take home. You get a quiet room. They get the best part of their week.
