Twice the job, in a world still built for the old one. That's the 4P Dad. Author Jason Wilson calls him the comprehensive man. Work, school and the pediatrician's office haven't caught up to him yet.

The relic at home

Two full-time jobs. One person still running the home.

For generations, moms carried most of the work of running the home, because most of them weren't also working full time. Now they are. When both parents work full time, both parents should be running the home. The data says that still isn't happening, and that gap is a relic, just like the 3:00 school bell and the unpaid leave.

54% vs 6%

In homes where both parents work full time, 54% say Mom does more of managing the kids' schedules. Only 6% say Dad does.

Pew Research Center, 2015

4.5 hrs

Even when husbands and wives earn about the same, wives spend about 4.5 more hours a week on caregiving and housework, while husbands work only about 3 more paid hours. Husbands also get about 3.5 more hours of leisure.

Pew Research Center, 2023 (American Time Use Survey)

3x

Dads now spend nearly three times as much time with their kids as dads did in 1965. The gap is closing, and dads are the ones closing it.

Pew Research Center, 2013
